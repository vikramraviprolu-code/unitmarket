### High

#### OD-1 Capture claim can leave no read-back operation (lane 05a, FIX-PAY2 H-1)

- Issue: if the process dies between the capture claim and the post-capture read, the order stays `capture_claimed`, no read-back operation exists, and `runDue` never picks it up. The buyer has been charged and nothing unlocks.
- Risk: paid order never settles without an operator; the money-path invariant "a claimed capture is always read back" does not hold.
- Test: `tests/mvp-a/open-defects.integration.test.ts` (skipped until FIX-PAY2 merges). Unskipped on this branch it fails with `orders.status = 'capture_claimed'`, expected `paid`.
- Fix: FIX-PAY2 (commit 9d3a34f on `fix/payments-pass2`). Status: open until merged and the test is unskipped and green.

#### OD-2 Refund accepted by PayPal but never read back (lane 05b, FIX-PAY2 H-2)

- Issue: if `getRefund` fails once after PayPal accepted the refund, the refund request stays `pending` for ever: no ledger refund entry, entitlement not refunded, access not ended.
- Test: same file. Unskipped it fails with `refund_requests.state = 'pending'`, expected `completed`.
- Fix: FIX-PAY2 (same commit). Status: open until merged and the test is unskipped and green.

#### F-2 Dependency audit reports two high advisories in postcss (via next)

- Issue: `corepack pnpm audit --prod` exits 1: 4 vulnerabilities (2 high, 2 moderate), all `postcss` under `apps__web > next > postcss`: GHSA-fxqj-rqcc-2cmp family, patched in postcss 8.5.12, 8.5.18 and 8.5.23 or later.
- Risk: attacker-controlled CSS or source-map input can read files or traverse paths when postcss processes it. Here postcss runs at build time on this repository's own CSS; no request path feeds it. Judged low in practice.
- Impact: none observed on money, access or secrets. Process: SUP-2 (zero high or a dated waiver) is not met.
- Fix: the owner records a dated waiver in `docs/decision-log.md` (suggest until the MVP-B gate), or the dependency owner raises `next` or adds a postcss override of 8.5.23 or later (needs the D-008 dependency decision) and re-runs the audit. Owner: coordinator and Vikram, before the MVP-B start.

- Status: open
