### Medium

#### F-1 No path to put the approval row and the switch-on on the hosted database (O-C8)

- Issue: `safety:approve` and `safety:resume` open the PGlite folder, which the running app holds; the single-opener lock (CO-9) makes them refuse. No maintenance start and no console approval exists. Read in `docs/design/hosting.md` step 3 and `mvpb-unseal-and-mvpc-offline.md` 7.1.
- Risk: the hosted app cannot make any PayPal call, so C-01, C-06 and C-07 cannot be proven on the host. Fails closed.
- Impact: availability of the owner window only. No money or secret exposure.
- Fix: the owner picks option (a) maintenance start or (b) operator-console approval; lane CO-7b is written and built; then Step 6 gets commands.
- Files: `docs/design/hosting.md`, `docs/owner/MVPC-OWNER-STEPS.md` (Step 6)
- Status: open, owner decision pending

#### F-2 The webhook certificate pin is empty and its learning method is undecided (ASM-64)

- Issue: `EXPECTED_CERT_SUBJECTS` is empty. Under `mvp_c_refunds` the webhook stays off while it is empty (tested in `apps/web/src/webhook.integration.test.ts`), so a first genuine delivery cannot teach the app the pin.
- Risk: C-01 cannot pass; PayPal's deliveries get 503 and retry for 3 days (no loss).
- Impact: availability of the webhook path only.
- Fix: the owner chooses Option A (a time-limited learn window recorded in the decision log) or Option B (an owner-run fetch script). Neither is built; the assistant did not choose.
- Files: `packages/paypal/src/webhook-cert.ts`, `apps/web/src/server/webhook.ts`, `docs/owner/MVPC-OWNER-STEPS.md` (Step 7)
- Status: open, owner decision pending

#### F-3 A backup on the host needs the app stopped (same cause as F-1)

- Issue: `scripts/backup.mjs` and `restore.mjs` refuse while the app holds the folder. On Render nothing stops the app and keeps the disk.
- Risk: the hosted half of C-10 and REL-5 (backup every 15 min, copied off the host) has no executable procedure. A Render disk snapshot is the fallback named in the CO-10 spec.
- Impact: recoverability on the host.
- Fix: decided together with F-1.
- Files: `scripts/backup.mjs`, `scripts/restore.mjs`
- Status: open, owner decision pending

### Low

#### F-4 C-11 resolves about one capture lease after the restart, not after two passes from the restart

- Issue: a capture that was sent and never answered carries a lease (`operation_lease_ms`, 60 s). Recovery leaves an operation whose lease runs to a live process (ASM-05B-1); the sweeper takes it when the lease ends. In the C-11 run the job loop made hundreds of no-op passes (200 ms each in test mode) before the lease ended, then resolved the order within 2 passes of that moment. nfr.md REL-2 says "within 2 reconciler passes" and names no time.
- Risk: after a crash the buyer sees "Confirming payment" for about a minute longer than a plain reading of REL-2 suggests. No double capture is possible (the same-key rule holds, one capture was sent).
- Impact: buyer-visible delay only.
- Fix: the owner accepts "lease end plus 2 passes" as the meaning of REL-2, or shortens the lease with a reviewed change (a shorter lease raises the chance of two processes owning one operation).
- Files: `domain/payments/src/recover.ts`, `domain/payments/src/capture.ts`, `scripts/c11-restart.integration.test.ts`
- Status: open, owner to accept

#### F-5 A refusal made by the route wrapper is neither audited nor logged

- Issue: probed on the real app: an operator without MFA posting a refund gets 403 `mfa_required` with 0 new `audit_log` rows and 0 log lines. `packages/security/src/route.ts` writes no audit row and logs only a thrown error. The same holds for 401, 413 and 429 (the webhook route's own refusals are audited: C-02).
- Risk: an attempted refund by an unverified operator leaves no trace. P9 is enforced; the record is missing.
- Impact: audit trail (SOP 17).
- Fix: an audit row for 403 on operator routes, in a later lane.
- Files: `packages/security/src/route.ts`
- Status: open

#### F-6 Known open items carried from the register

- ASM-61 (open, owner to accept): a partial refund never revokes an item, even when its amount equals one item's price in a multi-item unit. MVP-C units hold one item, so the offline rows do not meet it.
- ASM-68 (open): no hard floor of calls is kept for a new checkout; money work may spend the daily capture reserve to zero. Not built.
- `offer_refund` recovery cases only request a refund. Nothing executes those requests and each blocks its unit with `refund_in_flight` until an operator withdraws it (ASSUMPTIONS, open items). Before any route calls `resolveCase`, `ResolveDeps` needs an `executeRefund`.
- Wrapper-level refusals are not audited: F-5.
- ASM-57 (unverified): `UMS_TRUSTED_PROXIES=1` for Render. Checked on the host in Step 3, before the webhook is switched on.
- ASM-53 (unverified): GET order lists refunds under `purchase_units[].payments.refunds[]`. Seen in Step 10 or not at all.
- ASM-63, ASM-65, ASM-66, ASM-69: held or open as registered; ASM-66 must be closed before the webhook goes live (CO-2b), see the register.
- Status: open

- C-01, C-13, the real parts of C-06, C-07 and C-10, the ZAP baseline, the runbook dry run, the accessibility checks, the performance targets on the host and the proxy depth (ASM-57) were not tested.

- The C-08 switch-on check uses the actor the app resolves from a signed-in session and the safety package's `setSwitch`. No web route turns the switch on; the only other path is the owner's terminal command `safety:resume`, which this gate did not run.
- One lint exemption was added, as the spec allows: `sbx/no-package-escape` for six files under `tests/mvp-c` (the web composition is reachable only by relative path). It switches off no other rule.
