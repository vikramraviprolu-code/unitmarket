# Publisher side: requirements traceability matrix (31 Oct 2026)

Status: read only audit. Written against `origin/main` at `ded0380` in a separate worktree. No product code was changed, no PayPal call
was made, no env file was read and no credential was held. Every BUILT row was checked by opening the page, route, domain function or
test named in the evidence column; the hand over note was not taken on trust. Simulation and fixture results prove the modelled cases
only, not PayPal compatibility.

Question from the owner (Sakthi): "what happened to all the publisher requirements ... have you ensured all the requirements are
getting built or are already built", and "publisher related features on both the app as well as on the interface with respect to
sandbox people to be paid".

Sources: `docs/proposals/publisher-side-proposal-2026-10-30.md` (sections 3 to 8, prefix P), `docs/owner/DECISIONS-FOR-VIKRAM.md`
items 27 to 37 (prefix DV), PAY-01, PAY-07 and PAY-11 from the simulation repository `docs/dev/01-mvp-requirements.md` and the
simulation seller screens in `docs/specs/sim/sim-app-ui.md` section 6 (reference only, prefix REF), the owner's words (prefix OW), and
the money truth checks asked for in the audit brief (prefix MT).

Status words:

- BUILT: on `main`, with a test or a route that shows it.
- PARTIAL: some of it is on `main`; the missing part is named.
- NOT BUILT: nothing on `main` does it (a branch may exist).
- HUMAN-ONLY: only a person may do it (account, approval row, decision, deploy).
- DEFERRED-BY-DECISION: left out on purpose by a recorded decision or by the proposal's own LATER or cut list.

Effort: S under 1 lane hour, M one lane (60 to 90 minutes), L more than one lane. "Decision" says whether an owner decision is needed
before the next step.
