# DP-5: composite in the web app: pack selection, two PayPal approvals with progress, per-item outcomes

Task: let a buyer choose a two-publisher pack, approve once in UnitMarket, approve at PayPal once per publisher with "1 of 2" progress, and see per-item outcomes, library, receipt, case choices and the ops report, all from stored data.
Package: `apps/web` (including `composition.server.ts` and `src/server/dryrun-model.ts`) Owner: Sonnet 5.5 builder, effort high (holds composition) Reviewer: Opus 5.5 high (routes and composition), screenshot review for copy; Sakthi merges
Wave: MVP-D W4, after DP-3 and DP-4 merge. Prerequisites: OQ-1, OQ-5, OQ-8, OQ-11, OQ-17. Requirements: D-01 to D-03 and D-07 (user-visible side), P6, P13, P16, A11Y-4, A11Y-7, COMP-4, PAY-10.

Goal. **Pack (OQ-17):** `/discover` results get a checkbox per item and a "Buy selected together" button (up to 2 publishers, one currency; the server decides, the page only explains `mixed_currency` and `too_many_publishers` in plain words); it posts the existing `proposal` selection with several `offer_ids`. **Approve:** `/approve/[id]` shows one block per publisher (display name, items, subtotal), the total, and "You will approve 2 payments at PayPal, one for each publisher. You are charged only after you have approved both." (count from `snapshot.settlement.payments`). **Checkout:** `POST /api/orders/[ref]/checkout` returns `{ approve_url, progress }`. **Between approvals (OQ-5):** `/checkout/return` calls `onBuyerReturn`; while a provider order still awaits the buyer it renders "Step 2 of 2: approve your payment to <publisher> at PayPal" with one button to that stored approve URL (no automatic redirect), plus "Cancel the whole order"; never an id from the URL. **Cancel:** new `/checkout/cancel?ref=` page (PayPal's cancel URL; it does not exist today) and `POST /api/orders/[ref]/cancel` (buyer, CSRF, idempotent) calling `cancelCheckout`; copy "Nothing was charged." **Dry run:** `/checkout/dry-run/[order]` shows one approve step per provider order with its own scenario selector, so `happy` then `declined` gives a partial offline; the model refuses a reused `invoice_id` for one payee with `DUPLICATE_INVOICE_ID` (as the owner's account does, D-033). **Order page:** one line per item: "Ready in your library", "Not charged: PayPal declined this payment", "Confirming payment", "On hold while we check it", in an `aria-live="polite"` region. **Library (OQ-11):** one entry per item, each with "Bought together with <title>" and the order link. **Receipt:** DP-4 sections per publisher. **Help/case:** a `partial_composite` case shows the two choices as buttons via `POST /api/cases/[id]/choice` (buyer, CSRF, idempotent) calling `chooseCompositeOutcome`; default text says what happens if the buyer does nothing. **Ops:** the per-publisher report (MFA operator). **Composition:** binds `cancelCheckout`, `chooseCompositeOutcome`, `publisherReport`; offline seeding gives every publisher a distinct fake payee as today; sandbox seeding (OQ-1 = B) sets the owner's merchant id on `harbour-ledger` and `quayside-gazette` only where none is stored, and the ops page states "Both publishers are paid into the owner's sandbox account (…<last 4>)" from that data. Labels come from `environment`.

Inputs: DP-1 to DP-4 engine functions and views, `apps/web/src/**`, `apps/web/e2e/support.ts`, `scripts/route-inventory.mjs`, existing CO-8 copy rules.

Outputs: the DP-5 files in the overview; tests `apps/web/src/composite.state.test.ts`, `apps/web/src/composite.adversarial.test.ts`, `apps/web/src/composite-copy.golden.test.ts` with `src/golden/composite-copy.golden.txt`, `apps/web/e2e/composite.spec.ts`; the route inventory entries for the two new routes.
