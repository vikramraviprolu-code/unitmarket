## How to read it

Status words (one per row):

- built and merged: on `main` at `7869114`, with a test, a route or a document that shows it. For an owner decision item it means
  the decision is recorded or the work it asks for is on `main`.
- partial: some of it is on `main`; the row names the missing part.
- not built: nothing on `main` does it. A branch may exist; the row names it.
- human only: only a person may do it (account, approval row, secret value, deploy, decision, legal approval).
- deferred: left out on purpose by a decision, by an owner instruction or by a cut list.

"Where verified" names the suite (unit, state, contract, integration, adversarial, golden), the browser suite (e2e, Playwright),
a gate or evidence file, or a grep this lane ran. This lane ran no test suite (docs only lane, overnight assumption 17). A suite
named here is the one that holds the check; its result on `7869114` was not re-run by this lane.

Id scheme: lane ids (for example `PG-1`) for whole lanes; `R-<family>-<n>` for requirements added by the overnight work; the
traceability ids (`P-`, `DV-`, `PAY-`, `REF-`, `OW-`, `MT-`) unchanged. `DV-n` is item n of `DECISIONS-FOR-VIKRAM.md`; "assumption
n" is row n of `OVERNIGHT-ASSUMPTIONS.md`.

Id collisions to know about: the traceability matrix (section 3) planned lanes named PUB-E1 (settlement per block) and PUB-E2
(one settlement sentence), and the publisher questions memo (section 8) reused PUB-E2 and PUB-E3 for other work. The lanes that
merged used these names instead: SETTLE-A did the settlement per block and the one sentence; PUB-E1 did CSV, refunds view, since
your last visit and create offer; PUB-E2 did applicants; PUB-F1 did the sales totals and the cross check. This register uses the
merged names.
