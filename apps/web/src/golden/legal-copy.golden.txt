/legal/terms | title: Terms of purchase
/legal/terms | updated: Drafted 30 October 2026
/legal/terms | heading: About these terms
/legal/terms | text: These terms say what you agree to when you buy a unit on UnitMarket. They are a draft written for the owners of this demo to read and approve. They are not legal advice, and they may change before the demo is submitted.
/legal/terms | heading: What a unit is
/legal/terms | text: A unit is one article, or one part of a publisher's content, that you buy on its own instead of paying for a whole subscription. Before you pay, the offer shows what the unit is, what it costs and what access it gives.
/legal/terms | heading: Personal use only
/legal/terms | text: A unit is for your personal use only. You may not copy it for others, resell it, post it in public or share your access with another person. Redistribution of any kind is not allowed. Publishers keep their rights in their content.
/legal/terms | heading: How long you have access
/legal/terms | text: Access lasts five days from verified payment and ready delivery. The five days start once your payment is verified and the content is ready to deliver, whichever happens later. If we detect a delivery failure on our side, the lost time is credited back once delivery works again.
/legal/terms | text: Access to a unit ends early if a full refund is made for that unit. The refund policy explains how refunds work.
/legal/terms | heading: Price and terms are held for 15 minutes
/legal/terms | text: Once you start to buy, the price and the terms you were shown are held for 15 minutes. Nothing is charged until you approve the payment at PayPal. If you leave without paying, your unpaid order stays open until you cancel it or it expires, and you can continue to payment or cancel it from the order page. When the held price has gone, ask for a price again; the price or terms may be different the next time.
/legal/terms | heading: How you pay
/legal/terms | text: You pay through PayPal. There is one PayPal payment for each publisher. If your order has content from two publishers, it is paid in two separate PayPal payments, one for each publisher.
/legal/terms | heading: Test money only
/legal/terms | text: This is a test build. No real money moves. Payments use PayPal's sandbox with test money, or, in a dry run, nothing contacts PayPal at all. Nothing you buy here has real value.
/legal/privacy | title: Privacy notice
/legal/privacy | updated: Drafted 30 October 2026
/legal/privacy | heading: About this notice
/legal/privacy | text: This notice says what data this demo processes and why. It is a draft written for the owners to read and approve. It is not legal advice, and it may change before the demo is submitted.
/legal/privacy | heading: What we process
/legal/privacy | text: A session cookie, which keeps you signed in as the test persona you chose.
/legal/privacy | text: An appearance cookie named um_skin. It holds only the name of the skin you chose, and nothing about you. It is set only when you choose a skin, and it lasts 180 days.
/legal/privacy | text: A hashed network address. We do not keep your network address itself. We keep a hash of it, so we can limit how fast requests arrive and keep the demo safe.
/legal/privacy | text: The persona you chose, your orders, and your access events, such as when access to a unit started and when it ended.
/legal/privacy | text: What you type to describe what you need, and the quotes and approvals made from it. If the AI helper is switched on, that text is sent to an AI model provider. It never receives payment data.
/legal/privacy | text: PayPal processes your payment details on its own pages. We never see card data. Payer email addresses and names are redacted from our logs.
/legal/privacy | heading: Why we process it
/legal/privacy | text: We use this data only to run the demo. That means signing you in, keeping a record of what you bought and for how long, showing your library, limiting abuse, and looking into a problem with an order.
/legal/privacy | heading: How long we keep it
/legal/privacy | text: The design keeps logs for 30 days at the host and records of calls to the payment provider for 90 days. Deletion after these periods is not automated yet. Orders, the ledger and database backups are kept for the judging period. Some records, such as receipts, may need to be kept as financial records.
/legal/privacy | heading: Export or deletion
/legal/privacy | text: You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask for it to be deleted, through the operator. An operator runs a script on request; it is not a button in the app. The copy is a file of your own rows, such as your name, your briefs, your orders and your receipts. Deletion replaces your name and sign-in subject with a pseudonym, removes the text of your briefs and ends your sessions. Orders, receipts, the ledger, help cases and the audit log are kept as records and are not changed. That includes the text you wrote in a help case request, because the history of a help case cannot be edited.
/legal/privacy | heading: What we do not do
/legal/privacy | text: There is no advertising, no tracking scripts and no third-party fonts on this site. This site does not load scripts, fonts or images from other sites.
/legal/privacy | heading: Contact
/legal/privacy | text: Contact details will be added by the owners before submission.
/legal/refunds | title: Refund policy
/legal/refunds | updated: Drafted 30 October 2026
/legal/refunds | heading: About this policy
/legal/refunds | text: This policy says how refunds work in this demo. It is a draft written for the owners to read and approve. It is not legal advice, and it may change before the demo is submitted.
/legal/refunds | heading: Who decides
/legal/refunds | text: Refunds are decided by an operator, for each unit. Nothing is refunded automatically, and there is no button for buyers to refund themselves. There is no refund request form yet. If an item did not open, choose "Problem with this item?" on its page in your library. An operator reads help cases about an order and decides whether a refund is right.
/legal/refunds | heading: A full refund
/legal/refunds | text: A full refund of one publisher's part of your order ends access to what you bought from that publisher. Links to it stop working. A copy you already downloaded cannot be recalled.
/legal/refunds | heading: A partial refund
/legal/refunds | text: A partial refund does not end access to that publisher's part of your order, unless partial refunds together reach the full amount paid for it. It is treated as a price adjustment for the order.
/legal/refunds | heading: When a refund takes effect
/legal/refunds | text: After an operator decides a refund, it is requested from PayPal. It is booked when PayPal confirms it. Until then your access does not change.
/legal/refunds | heading: Test money only
/legal/refunds | text: This is a test build. A refund returns test money to a test account, or, in a dry run, nothing is sent to PayPal. No real money moves.

/legal/publisher-terms | text: Each publisher has a statement page. It adds up the stored records of each payment: sales, refunds and reversals. The PayPal fee is shown beside them when PayPal reported it, and is not subtracted.

/legal/publisher-terms | text: This is a test build. No real money moves. Payments use PayPal's sandbox with test money, or, in a dry run, nothing contacts PayPal at all. Nothing here is a live payment, a real sale or an offer to sell anything of real value.
