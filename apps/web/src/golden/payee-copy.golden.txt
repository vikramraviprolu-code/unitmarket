heading: Where you get paid

labels: Account | Verified | Verified by | Settlement

account set: Account ending AB12.
verified none: Not verified yet.
verified at: Verified on WHEN.
settlement none: No payee account is set, so your offers cannot be sold yet.
settlement waiting: Sales cannot settle until the account ending AB12 is verified.
settlement verified: Dry run sales are recorded against the account ending AB12. No real money moves.
settlement verified sandbox: Sandbox sales are recorded against the account ending AB12. No real money moves.
owner only: Payee changes on the hosted app are made by the owner.
