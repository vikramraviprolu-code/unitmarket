The problem

Small purchases do not fit how content and payments are sold today.

A reader
Needs one article. Is asked for a whole subscription.

So the reader leaves, and the publisher earns nothing from a person who was ready to pay.

An AI agent
Buys a 7.6¢ brief. Pays it as its own PayPal payment.

6.5 times the content

UnitMarket
The idea: three pillars

Group the demand, group the supply, make cents worth paying.

1

Buyer consolidation

Many buyers who want the same unit share one payment and unlock a volume price.

Simulated
10,000 single-unit orders became 233 payments to publishers.
2

Seller consolidation

Small publishers pool on a topic to fill bundles no single publisher could fill alone.

Simulated
33 bundle requests: 0 filled alone, 33 filled pooled.
3

Micro-transactions

Agents trade in 5 to 10 cent pieces. A ledger nets them into a few PayPal movements.

Agent caps builtLedger simulated
10,000 trades settled in 198 PayPal movements instead of 10,000.
